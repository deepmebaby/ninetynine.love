How it works

Three steps. We do two of them.

1

You talk

Just talk. We get you.

It feels like texting a friend who’s genuinely curious about your week. No forms, no checkboxes, no bio. While you talk, we quietly learn two things: who you are, and who you’re looking for. The more you talk, the better we know you.

2

We do the work

We find your fit. Not your clone.

Other apps match you with people just like you: same hobbies, same answers. Chemistry doesn’t work like that. We search every day, near you, for the person who’s looking for someone exactly like you, and who happens to be what you’re looking for. Two different people, one fit. They say yes too, so every match is a yes from both sides.

You only have to be yourself. We’ll find the person who’s looking for exactly that.

3

Your 1%

Go find them.

You both said yes. That’s it: no chat first, no planning, no “so… when are you free?”. You get a rough area on the map where they are. Walk toward it and the area shrinks. Keep going, it shrinks again. Close enough, and there they are: a real face, right in front of you. We leave this part to you on purpose. It’s the best part.

Why we do this

People need people. Not screens.

A match on a screen is not a date. A coffee is. A walk is. A laugh across a table is. Real chemistry happens in the same room, not in a chat window. So we do everything that keeps you on your phone, and we give you back the only part that matters: the moment you meet.

Put the phone down. Go meet someone.

Questions

Good to know.

Who am I talking to?
Think of it as a friend who’s weirdly good at setting people up. It listens, asks the right questions and remembers the details. (Yes, it’s an AI. No, it doesn’t judge.)
Who sees my profile?
Nobody scrolls through people on ninetynine. Someone sees your profile only when we think you two fit.
Is my location private?
Yes. Nobody ever sees your exact location, not even a match. When you both say yes, they see a rough area, a neighbourhood, not a pin. As you walk toward each other, the area shrinks. It only gets precise when you’re both almost there, and it fades again when you leave.
